# Supply Chain Coordination & Procurement System

Operational procurement and workforce planning system built using Excel, Power Query, and supply chain coordination workflows to support purchasing visibility, inventory tracking, staffing demand planning, and vendor coordination for commercial service operations.

---

## Overview

This system was developed to improve operational purchasing coordination and resource planning across multiple commercial cleaning accounts by centralizing:

- supply ordering
- staffing demand visibility
- vendor coordination
- inventory forecasting
- contract support planning
- operational purchasing workflows

The workbook was structured around real operational management processes used to monitor staffing requirements, recurring supply needs, and procurement planning across multiple service locations.

---

## Operational Purpose

The system supports day-to-day management operations by helping coordinate:

- recurring consumable purchases
- contract supply forecasting
- staffing-related inventory demand
- vendor tracking
- operational readiness across multiple locations

The workflow reduces manual tracking and improves visibility into operational purchasing decisions tied directly to staffing activity and contract demand.

---

## Key Features

### Procurement Coordination
- Tracks recurring operational supply requirements
- Supports purchasing visibility across multiple accounts
- Organizes vendor and supply planning workflows

### Inventory Forecasting
- Connects staffing demand to projected supply usage
- Helps identify future purchasing needs
- Reduces operational shortages and reactive ordering

### Vendor Management
- Centralizes vendor-related operational data
- Supports purchasing coordination and supply planning
- Improves operational organization for procurement activity

### Workforce-Linked Planning
- Aligns supply planning with staffing schedules and contract demand
- Improves operational preparedness across active job sites

### Operational Reporting
- Provides simplified operational visibility for managers
- Supports quick review of purchasing and staffing coordination activity
